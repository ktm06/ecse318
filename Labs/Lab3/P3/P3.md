I know this is correct because my waveform in AssignmentWaveform.png lines up with the waveform expected in the packet. SSPFSSOUT goes high for one SSPCLKOUT period on the last bit (LSB) of each byte, at 820 ps and 1460 ps, and the next byte's MSB starts right as the pulse ends, at 900 ps and 1540 ps. The 8 bits from 900 ps to 1540 ps are 0x0F (0000 1111), sent MSB first, and SSPOE_B stays low the whole time, the same as nSSPOE (=0) in the figure.

The FIFOs also behave the way the packet asks, as shown in OutputWaveform.png. SSPTXINTR goes high at 260 ps when the TxFIFO fills up, so the bytes written while it is high (0xF3, 0xB6 and the first 0x84) are ignored, and it drops at 860 ps when the transmitter takes the next byte. SSPRXINTR goes high at 3460 ps once the RxFIFO holds four bytes. At the end, data_out shows 0x51, the third byte that was written, which means the first two bytes (0x94 and 0x0F) were read back out in order.
